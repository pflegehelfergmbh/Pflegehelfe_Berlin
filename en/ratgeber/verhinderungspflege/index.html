Care guide · For carers

When a family carer needs a break

If someone who normally cares for a relative becomes temporarily unavailable, replacement care (Verhinderungspflege) may be an option under certain conditions.

When might replacement care be relevant?

Verhinderungspflege means temporary replacement care when someone who usually provides unpaid or family care cannot do so for reasons such as illness, a holiday or other circumstances. The relevant statutory eligibility conditions must be met.

How should families prepare?

  • The dates and hours for which cover is needed
  • Which tasks the usual carer undertakes
  • Important routines, medical information and relevant contacts
  • Which benefit conditions and possible reimbursements your Pflegekasse has confirmed

Who can provide temporary cover?

Who may take over care and which costs can be reimbursed depend on the individual case and current legal rules. Contact your Pflegekasse for binding information. This guide does not promise that Malaika provides Verhinderungspflege as a separate service.
