Household support: what to consider

Shopping, preparing meals and keeping up with chores can become more difficult. A clear picture of what is needed helps families find suitable support.

What is household assistance?

When shopping, preparing meals, cleaning or doing laundry becomes difficult, additional support can help with everyday life. The specific tasks depend on the person's requirements and what a provider offers.

How do I assess the need?

  • Which everyday chores have become difficult
  • How frequently help is required
  • Which tasks relatives already manage
  • Whether any long-term care insurance benefits may apply

What should I ask a potential provider?

Different services offer different types of household help. Ask which tasks are included, what they cost and whether your Pflegekasse may contribute. This article offers general information; it does not confirm that Malaika provides the household services described.
