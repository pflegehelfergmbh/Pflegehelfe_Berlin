Our services · Personal care

Personal care in the comfort of home

When washing, dressing or moving around becomes difficult, practical support can help someone remain safe and comfortable in familiar surroundings.

When is personal care helpful?

Personal care can support people who have difficulty washing, bathing, dressing or moving around their home. The assistance should respect what the person is still able to do independently.

What tasks may be included?

  • Assistance with washing, bathing and showering
  • Help getting dressed and undressed
  • Support with essential movement around the home
  • Other agreed activities involved in personal care

How is care arranged?

When enquiring, describe which tasks are difficult, where the person lives and when support is needed. Visiting times, frequency and scope depend on individual circumstances and service capacity.

Who pays for personal care?

If the requirements for eligible home care benefits are met, Pflegekasse may pay up to the applicable statutory limit for the person's Pflegegrad. Additional services or uncovered costs can result in personal contributions.

Understand funding for both types of care

What should I prepare for the first conversation?

Make a note of which tasks are difficult at different times of day, which relatives already help and the exact care address.

Does having help mean giving up independence?

No. Support should focus on tasks that actually require assistance, while respecting established routines and what the person can do for themselves.
