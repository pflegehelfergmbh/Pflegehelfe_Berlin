Our services · Home nursing

Nursing care prescribed by your doctor

Some nursing procedures can be carried out at home when a doctor has prescribed them. The specific tasks, timing and availability need to be arranged individually.

What is Behandlungspflege?

Treatment-related nursing at home involves medical nursing tasks that may be required on a doctor's prescription. Examples of home nursing may include wound care, certain injections or support with prescribed medicines. Whether Malaika can carry out any specific task must be checked individually.

What should I have ready?

  • Details of the prescribed nursing procedure and how long it is needed
  • The doctor's prescription, if already available
  • The address where care is required
  • The desired start date and visiting times

Will my Krankenkasse pay?

The statutory health insurance fund may cover medically necessary home nursing if the legal requirements are met. Adults can still be required to pay statutory co-payments unless exempt. A blanket promise that every treatment is completely free would be inaccurate.

Understand funding for nursing and personal care

Can I arrange home nursing without a prescription?

For treatment covered under statutory health insurance, a relevant medical prescription or another qualifying legal basis is generally required. Ask your Krankenkasse about your circumstances.

Can care be coordinated with my doctor?

Coordination with the treating practice may be important. Sharing medical information and obtaining any necessary consent are agreed for the individual situation.
