Our services

The right support for everyday life

Malaika provides two main types of domiciliary care: personal care (Grundpflege) and medically prescribed home nursing (Behandlungspflege). Find out how they differ and how funding works.

Confirmed services

Two types of support at home

Personal care helps with basic daily activities. Prescribed nursing care addresses medical nursing requirements.

✳

Personal care (Grundpflege)

Help with washing, dressing, mobility and other daily personal tasks.

Learn about personal care
✳

Prescribed nursing (Behandlungspflege)

Nursing procedures needed at home on a doctor's prescription, subject to availability.

Learn about prescribed nursing

Which kind of care might you need?

If everyday washing, dressing or moving around has become difficult, personal care may be appropriate. For medical nursing procedures ordered by a doctor, ask about Behandlungspflege. The exact services and visiting times are agreed individually.

Costs and funding

Who pays for my treatment and care?

Prescribed nursing · Krankenkasse

When medically necessary and prescribed by a doctor, home nursing may be paid for by the health insurance fund if the statutory conditions are met. Adult patients may have to make statutory co-payments unless exempt.

Personal care · Pflegekasse and private contributions

Long-term care insurance may contribute to eligible care depending on the assessed Pflegegrad, individual entitlement and statutory benefit limits. Costs beyond the amount covered may be payable privately.

Your health or long-term care insurance fund makes the final decision on eligibility and payments in your circumstances. We cannot guarantee that all care will be free of charge.

Official information: German Federal Ministry of Health — home care benefits (German).
