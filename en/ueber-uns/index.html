About Malaika

Good care begins with listening

Everyone has their own routines, preferences and needs. We believe good care respects the individual and helps them stay in familiar surroundings.

Respect for each person's independence

Everyone has different needs, wishes and habits. Care planning should focus on support that is genuinely needed while respecting abilities and routines that matter to the person.

What Malaika provides

Pflegedienst Malaika GmbH provides personal care (Grundpflege) and prescribed nursing care (Behandlungspflege) at home. The exact services and visiting schedule are agreed with regard to individual needs and staff availability.

Discover our confirmed services

Our multilingual team

Being understood is particularly important in personal care. Our team speaks four languages:

  • Deutsch
  • Русский
  • English
  • Français

Let us know your preferred language when you enquire, so suitable staffing can be discussed for the required visits.

Care visits across Berlin

Our mobile team travels by car to people's homes throughout the stated Berlin service areas. The company's office is at Flankenschanze 1 in Berlin-Spandau.
