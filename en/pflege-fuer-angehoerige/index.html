For families

“My parents need help at home.”

Perhaps your mum feels less steady in the shower, or your dad has been prescribed nursing care. Start by looking at the specific activities that are becoming difficult.

When your mum needs help washing or dressing

If showering, personal hygiene or dressing becomes difficult, Grundpflege may be relevant. Note which tasks can still be managed independently and where support is needed regularly.

Learn about personal care at home

When your dad needs prescribed nursing

Medical procedures such as wound treatment or certain injections must be arranged separately. Ask the treating doctor about the prescription and the care provider whether it can undertake the relevant procedure.

Learn about prescribed nursing care

If you care for a relative yourself

Sharing responsibilities can make a real difference. Who helps in the morning? Who shops for groceries? Who speaks with the medical team? Temporary replacement care may be an option when a regular family carer is unavailable.

Learn about Verhinderungspflege

Prepare for your first enquiry

  • The address where your parent lives
  • What help is needed in the morning, afternoon or evening
  • Whether a Pflegegrad or prescription is in place
  • When care should ideally start
  • Which tasks are currently managed by relatives
Can I enquire on behalf of my mother or father?

Yes. Relatives can explain the situation initially. The wishes and necessary consent of the person receiving care must be respected when making arrangements and sharing health information.

How quickly can care begin?

The possible start date depends on the care needs, address, relevant documents and availability. A specific start date can only be discussed individually.

What if my parent does not want outside help?

Try talking about particular difficulties and personal preferences. Agreeing on a small amount of practical help may be easier than proposing major changes all at once.
