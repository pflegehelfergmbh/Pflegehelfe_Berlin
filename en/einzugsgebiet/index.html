Areas we cover

Where our care workers travel

Pflegedienst Malaika provides home visits in these Berlin locations:

  • Wedding
  • Reinickendorf / Wittenau
  • Lichtenberg
  • Steglitz / Zehlendorf
  • Tempelhof
  • Charlottenburg
  • Spandau

Our staff have access to vehicles and travel to people's homes. Exact visiting times depend on care requirements and the available rota.

How to check availability at your address

Please tell us the street, postcode, type of care needed and desired start date. We can then discuss whether visits can be arranged within our current schedule. Our office location in Spandau does not restrict our work to that district.

Can I contact Malaika if I live in one of these districts?

Yes. Please give us the full address so we can check availability and possible visiting times.

Must I come to the office for care?

No. Domiciliary care takes place in the person's home. Any administrative appointments can be arranged separately if needed.
