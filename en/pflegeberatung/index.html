Understanding care in Germany

Making sense of care grades and funding

If you are organising support for someone you love, German insurance terminology may feel overwhelming. Here are the key concepts and where to obtain binding information.

What is a Pflegegrad?

A Pflegegrad is a German assessment category indicating the level of a person's long-term care needs. If ongoing support is required, you can apply to your Pflegekasse for an assessment. The fund explains the application process and potential benefits.

What is the difference between Pflegegeld and Pflegesachleistungen?

Both relate to German long-term care insurance but operate differently. Pflegegeld is generally associated with privately arranged care, while Pflegesachleistungen are for qualifying professional care services. The available combinations depend on the case and should be checked with the Pflegekasse.

How is medically prescribed care funded?

Medically necessary nursing care prescribed by a doctor may fall under statutory health insurance (Krankenkasse) rather than long-term care insurance. The required documents, eligibility criteria and possible co-payments depend on the circumstances.

Advisory visits under § 37(3) SGB XI

Some recipients of Pflegegeld must receive regular advisory visits. Your Pflegekasse can confirm whether this applies and at what intervals. Please ask Malaika directly whether it provides these visits; we do not present them as a confirmed service.

Useful information to prepare

  • Which everyday activities the person can no longer manage alone
  • Whether a Pflegegrad has been assessed or applied for
  • Any existing doctor's prescription
  • Which tasks relatives currently undertake
Can Malaika determine my Pflegegrad?

No. The relevant Pflegekasse makes decisions on the assessed care grade under the legal assessment procedure.

What if care is needed urgently?

For urgent medical needs, contact the relevant medical or emergency services. For continuing care, speak with the insurer and a care provider about practical arrangements.
