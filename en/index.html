What might care insurance cover?

Benefits depend on the care grade, type of help and remaining statutory benefit allowance.

Section 36, SGB XI: With care grades 2–5, certain personal care, supervision and household assistance can be covered within statutory limits.

Section 45b, SGB XI: In 2026, up to €131 per month may be available as a designated relief allowance for eligible home-based support.

Conditions vary. With care grades 2–5, this relief allowance cannot be used for certain personal self-care services. Uncovered costs may be payable privately.
