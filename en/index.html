Frequently asked questions

Who pays for my treatment and personal care?

Medically necessary home nursing prescribed by a doctor may be covered by Krankenkasse when the legal conditions are met; statutory co-payments may apply. Pflegekasse may contribute towards personal care depending on the assessed care grade and individual entitlement. Any uncovered costs may be payable privately.

Which Berlin districts does Malaika cover?

We provide visits in Wedding, Reinickendorf/Wittenau, Lichtenberg, Steglitz/Zehlendorf, Tempelhof, Charlottenburg and Spandau. Availability and visiting times at your address must be agreed individually.

Can I talk to the care team in English?

Our team speaks English, German, Russian and French. Tell us your preferred language when enquiring so suitable communication arrangements can be discussed.

How do I make my first enquiry?

Call us with the address, the support needed and your preferred start date. If a Pflegegrad or doctor's prescription is already available, mention it during the conversation.
